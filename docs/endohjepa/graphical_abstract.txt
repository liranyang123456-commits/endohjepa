GRAPHICAL ABSTRACT (design brief + caption)

Caption:
"Endo-HJEPA studies latent prediction across laparoscopy, GI
endoscopy and bronchoscopy without pixel decoding. A frozen V-JEPA 2 encoder
produces spatiotemporal tokens; a residual L1 forecaster is the evaluated
forecast model, while a separately audited deterministic SE(3)-conditioned branch evaluates
observed camera-motion associations and an oracle-goal retrieval proxy.
The SCARED audit gives 88.5% deranged-batch and 92.2% fixed-bank pair wins.
Near-wall risk does not meet its evaluation gate (AUC 0.523), and robot control
is not evaluated."

Layout (single column, left-to-right pipeline):
[3 scope icons: laparoscope / GI scope / bronchoscope]
   -> [frozen V-JEPA 2 ViT-L encoder] -> dense tokens
   -> [evaluated L1 residual; L2 implemented but not ablated]
   -> [audited SE(3)-conditioned branch]
   -> outputs: latent forecast | audited action association | retrieval diagnostic

Colour coding: inputs (orange), encoder (blue), prediction (green/yellow),
SE(3) evaluation (purple). Footer: "predict representations, not pixels."
