GRAPHICAL ABSTRACT (design brief + caption)

Caption:
"Endo-HJEPA learns a unified world model across laparoscopy, GI endoscopy, and
bronchoscopy by predicting plannable latent representations—not pixels. A frozen
V-JEPA 2 encoder produces dense spatio-temporal tokens; hierarchical L1/L2/L3
predictors forecast future latents; a contrastive energy head scores transition
plausibility and drives latent-space model-predictive control, flagging
collision-risk (near-wall) transitions."

Layout (single column, left-to-right pipeline):
[3 scope icons: laparoscope / GI scope / bronchoscope]
   -> [frozen V-JEPA 2 ViT-L encoder] -> dense tokens
   -> [L1 dense/causal -> L2 coarse -> L3 action-conditioned]
   -> [energy head + latent MPC]
   -> outputs: forecast | plan-to-goal | energy (collision-risk) gauge

Colour coding: inputs (orange), encoder (blue), hierarchy (green/yellow/red),
energy (purple), planning (indigo). Footer: "predict representations, not pixels."
