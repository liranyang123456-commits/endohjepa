Ethics approval and consent to participate
Public datasets were used under their original licences. The ION component is
a retrospective secondary analysis of de-identified clinical imaging,
comprising CT volumes and intra-operative bronchoscopy videos. The responsible
hospital department de-identified the clinical data through its institutional
review process, and Dr. Nan Wei provided the resulting research data. Data
access and use were reviewed and authorised through the institutional
data-governance process of Henan Provincial People's Hospital. No animal
experiments, prospective enrolment, research-directed intervention or
identifiable participant information were involved, and the study complies
with the Declaration of Helsinki.

Consent for publication
Not applicable; no identifiable individual data are presented.
