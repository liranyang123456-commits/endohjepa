# Private clinical-data ethics statement

> This study is a retrospective secondary analysis of de-identified clinical
> imaging, comprising in-house laparoscopic and intra-operative ION
> bronchoscopy videos. Associated CT volumes were available under the same
> governance process but were not used in the reported analyses. The
> clinical data were de-identified by the responsible hospital department
> through its institutional review process and were provided by Dr. Nan
> Wei. Data access and use were reviewed and authorised through the
> institutional data-governance process of Henan Provincial People's Hospital.
> The authors conducted no new animal experiments, prospective enrolment or
> research-directed intervention, and no identifiable participant information
> was used. The
> study was conducted in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki.

Before submission, the responsible institution must append the formal review
committee name, ethics approval or exemption identifier, decision date and
consent-waiver determination. These details are not available in the current
project records and must not be guessed.
